Notice Regarding Ephemeral Communications
Syphr is an ephemeral, peer-to-peer web chat. We do not store, archive, or back up any conversations, files, or audio notes. Once a session ends or the tab is closed, your messages are permanently erased. There is no technical recovery mechanism.
User Autonomy
You retain full responsibility for the data and files you choose to transmit to peers across the WebRTC connection.
Irreversible Eradication
Closing your browser or triggering the emergency NUKE protocol instantly and irreversibly destroys volatile session state.
No Server Intermediary
Messages travel directly between devices. Syphr operates as a client-side interface with no central message storage server.

Under certain network configurations (e.g., restrictive enterprise symmetric NATs or firewalls), peer connection establishment may fail without a relay server. Furthermore, because WebRTC establishes a direct connection between IP endpoints, connecting with another user inherently exposes your network endpoint to that user. Syphr strongly encourages the use of reputable VPNs if IP confidentiality from your peer is required.
